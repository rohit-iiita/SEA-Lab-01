# Failure Timeline: Healthcare.gov Launch Failure

## Overview

The Healthcare.gov case study describes the development, launch, and
recovery of a large federal health insurance marketplace system. The
timeline shows how project and technical problems accumulated before the
October 1, 2013 launch.

## Key Technical Interpretation

The timeline demonstrates that the launch failure was not caused by a
single event on October 1, 2013. Problems had accumulated throughout
development, particularly around requirements, schedule, testing,
management, integration, and capacity.

The most important warning sign was that the project was approaching
launch while major work remained unfinished. This reduced the
opportunity to identify and resolve problems before production.

## Key Lesson

A complex system should not be launched simply because a deadline has
arrived. Launch readiness should be based on evidence from development,
integration, testing, capacity validation, and risk assessment.
